Review the class, not the person

Write about the course, the workload, the teaching, and the grading. Comments about someone's appearance, personal life, or character are removed.

Say what you actually experienced

Rate courses you took and professors you had. Tag the semester so readers know how current your advice is.

No academic dishonesty

Study strategy is welcome. Exam questions, answer keys, and anything that violates the college's academic integrity policy are not.

Keep it a community

No harassment, no slurs, no spam, no impersonation. Report anything that crosses the line and a moderator will look at it.

Moderators can hide or remove content and warn or suspend accounts. These guidelines are a draft — the community lead finalizes them before launch.
