Cards and directory rows

The card is the default container. The row is the unit of every directory.

A plain card

.card is the border and radius; .card-pad adds the padding, so a card holding a full-bleed list can skip it.

A card holding rows

.row-link is the directory row used by the Courses and Professors tabs. The rating is the leftmost thing on the row on purpose.

Badges

Short status and category labels. Neutral by default; accent for emphasis; danger for moderation.
