Students who took a course two semesters ago know things the catalog never tells you — which professor to take, how heavy the workload really is, what the exams are like. That knowledge normally lives in group chats and then disappears. RamHub collects it, ties it to the real course and the real professor, and keeps it for the students who come next.

Access is limited to verified Farmingdale State College students. Around the course and professor directories sit a community feed, campus-info tabs, a personal filing cabinet for saving what matters, and an assistant that answers questions with links to its sources.

RamHub is a semester-long capstone project built by a team of six students. It is not an official Farmingdale State College website.
