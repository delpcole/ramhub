Group One: Project Ideas Proposal

1.	RamHub: Community Forum, account media posting, the RateMyProfessors-style core for specific classes, community feed, campus tabs, AI mascot assistant, AI parking assistant, AI syllabus assistant.

  1.1.	Existing need or problem - class knowledge disappears when students graduate; campus info is scattered; first-year/transfer/first-gen students hit hardest.
 
  1.2.	Target audience - current FSC undergrads, with advisors/prospective students secondary. 
  
  1.3.	Five epics -
  
    1.3.1.	Accounts & Moderation - Personalized accounts and dedicated moderation will allow students to use the app safely and professionally.
    
    1.3.2.	Course & Professor directories - A faster way to view current course offerings and professor information.
    
    1.3.3.	Advice Threads - Places for people to share resources and tips for one another.
    
    1.3.4.	Community Feed & Campus-info tabs - Direct feed of status updates at Farmingdale. Provides an easier place to access campus info.
    
    1.3.5.	Search, filing cabinet, & AI assistant - Allows students to search for any topics and save their favorite pages. The AI assistant can help with parking, scheduling, and reminders.
    
  1.4.	Technologies - In addition to the tech stack, we will need: HTML, CSS, JavaScript.
  
  1.5.	This is our first choice because we all felt passionate about creating a new place for students to connect with classmates and share advice, collaborate, and keep students' knowledge for future use.
    3.3.5.	Integrations & Collaboration - Connect the platform to external academic systems (Canvas, Blackboard, Moodle) for direct syllabus/LMS import and enable social features like sharing schedules with study groups or classmates to find overlapping free time. 
    
  3.4.	Technologies - In addition to the tech stack, we will need: django-allauth, Google Calendar API, django-storages.
